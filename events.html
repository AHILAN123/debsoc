Notes on our events

What events does DebSoc IIEST host?

BECON MUN is the flagship. The Paramesh Ranjan Dhar Memorial Debate, Disputation (the induction), Voices in Focus and Vox Populi (the freshers’ debate) run through the year.

With partners we run Face Off and VicharAgni (with Instruo), TechManthan (with the Mechanical Engineering Department), an Oxford-style debate at REBECA, and SHASTRARTH. Earlier years had IIEST MUN editions in 2016, 2018 and 2019.

What is Voices in Focus?

Voices in Focus is an online debate and speech event that anyone can join easily. It is aimed at including the whole student body: the point is to give every student a chance to speak and be heard.

What is the Paramesh Ranjan Dhar Memorial Debate?

It is held in memory of the late Paramesh Ranjan Dhar, an alumnus of erstwhile B.E. College, which is today’s IIEST Shibpur. The first edition on record is from 2018, and the chronicle lists the editions.

In his eulogy, Dr Hirak Sen writes that cancer struck him suddenly in 1982, that he fought it for twenty-two years and cleared it in 2004, and then lived another thirteen years celebrating life. “Any other person would have caved in much earlier, but not Parameshda.” He never complained about the illness to his friends.

The same eulogy remembers how he treated people. At a lunch in 1963 he quietly told a new junior engineer which spoon to use, so the junior was not embarrassed. Dr Sen calls it helping juniors with total dignity, and says he led his teams from the front.

How does BECON MUN fit into DebSoc’s events?

BECON MUN is our flagship Model United Nations conference. Its inaugural edition ran on 28–29 March 2026 with 122 delegates from 34 institutes and two committees, UNGA-DISEC and Lok Sabha. Read more on the BECON page.

Can outside students attend DebSoc events?

Some of them, yes. BECON MUN is open to students from colleges and universities across India. For anything else, ask at debsoc.iiest@gmail.com or see the contact page.
