Before you sign up

Who can become a member of DebSoc?

DebSoc is the Debating Society of IIEST Shibpur, and it inducts new members each year through Disputation. First years are especially encouraged. This directory lists people who have an account on this site.

Do I need experience to join?

No. Most members arrive with no debate or public speaking background. Sessions are set up to bring newcomers up to speed, and the handbook covers the basics.
