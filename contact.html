Finding DebSoc

What is DebSoc’s email address?

debsoc.iiest@gmail.com. Email is the quickest route for enquiries. Announcements go out on Instagram at @debsoc_iiests.

How do I propose a sponsorship or collaboration for BECON?

Email debsoc.iiest@gmail.com with who you are, what you are proposing and which edition you mean. See the BECON page for what the conference looked like in 2026.

Where is DebSoc on campus?

On the 2nd floor of the Students’ Amenities Centre, IIEST Shibpur, Botanic Garden, Howrah 711103, West Bengal. Our old home was Slater Hall.
