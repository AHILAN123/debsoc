Delegate’s notes

Reading the results

Who won Best Delegate in DISEC at BECON 2026?

Kinshuk Trivedi and Adrish Hossain, representing Belarus. High Commendations went to Karan Tripathi (Germany) and Sattwik Dhar Roy (Ukraine).

Who won Best Parliamentarian in Lok Sabha?

Arnab Ghosh and Annesha Mukherjee, who played Sarabjeet Singh Khalsa. Akshat Nishania and Sara Ghosh took the High Commendations.

What awards does BECON give?

In Lok Sabha: Best Parliamentarian, High Commendation, Special Mention, Outstanding Parliamentarian, Honorable Mention and Verbal Mention. In DISEC: Best Delegate, High Commendation, Special Mention and Honourable Mention.

How are delegates judged?

This page lists placings, not score sheets, so we will not invent a rubric for it. In most MUNs the chairs mark delegates on research, speaking, contribution to caucus and drafting, but criteria differ by conference.

The handbook explains how judging works in general.
