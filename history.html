What were IIEST’s earlier names?

IIEST Shibpur began as Bengal Engineering College (B.E. College), established in 1856. Counting from then, 2027 marks 171 years. It later became Bengal Engineering and Science University (BESU), and in March 2014 it was converted into an Institute of National Importance and renamed IIEST Shibpur.

The institute describes itself as India’s second-oldest engineering institution by year of graduation. Its annual cultural fest, REBECA, reached its 85th edition in March 2026, and IIEST calls it the oldest institute fest in India.

DebSoc has been active since 2009, so it has debated under the B.E. College and BESU names as well as IIEST. To learn more about the institute, visit the official website, iiests.ac.in.

signed, DebSoc

What are DebSoc’s first recorded events?

The chronicle starts in 2013, with weekly group discussion sessions and two IIEST freshers winning the open debate at IISER Kolkata. IIEST MUN 2016 is the first MUN on record.
